Sources. Lenia is Bert Wang-Chak Chan's: Lenia — Biology of Artificial Life, Complex Systems 28 (2019), arXiv:1812.05433. The kernel, growth functions, update and the Orbium pattern were taken from his reference code and catalogue (github.com/Chakazul/Lenia, Python/LeniaND.py and Python/animals.json), not from memory. My update agrees with a literal transcription of his to within 5×10−13.

How the plates run. Each specimen is computed live in your browser by a small JavaScript Lenia (fast Fourier transform, double precision) from the saved pattern and rule. Before publishing I ran all eighteen in both engines for 3,000 steps. Eleven orderly species agreed to within 10−11; the Pearl drifted to 3×10−8; the six chaotic species diverged cell by cell after 500 to 3,000 steps while agreeing on mean mass to within 0.3%. Plates animate only while on screen.

Files. The search, measurements, specimens and working notes sit beside this book in the garden folder: lenia.py, search.py, characterize.py, specimens/, search_run/ and NOTES.md, which records what went wrong along the way.
